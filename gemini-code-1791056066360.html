Burdekin Coast Beach 4WD Permitted

Alva Beach & Gutter Network

Prime beach 4WD run at low tide. Great surf gutters for Flathead, Whiting, and Dart. Creeks behind the spit yield solid Barramundi and seasonal Mud Crabs.

Top Baits/Lures: Prawn imitations, Live mullet, Yabbies
Best Conditions: Run-in tide, gentle SE winds
Beware of soft sand banks near creek outlets.
Estuary & Mangroves Boat Ramp & Shore Access

Groper Creek & River Mouth

Renowned crabbing waters and heavy snags holding trophy Mangrove Jack and Barramundi. Working the mangrove drains on the outgoing tide produces consistent action.

Top Baits/Lures: Fresh mullet frames (pots), Soft vibes
Best Conditions: Neap tides (slow current inside creek)
Croc country — always stay well back from banks.
National Park Headland 4WD Tracks / Boating

Cape Upstart National Park

Spectacular granite headlands where sheltered bays meet blue water. Excellent for pelagics like Queenfish, Trevally, and Spanish Mackerel, with Coral Trout on the shallow reef ledges.

Top Baits/Lures: Metal spoons, Garfish trolling rigs
Best Conditions: Early morning, light winds under 10 knots
Remote bush camping permits required via QPWS.
Townsville Inshore Estuary & Weirs

Ross River & Estuary Rockwalls

Accessible fishing right inside Townsville. The rockwalls and bridges produce Barra and Grunter at night, while upstream weirs are popular for light-tackle catch and release.

Top Baits/Lures: Live sardines, Paddle-tail plastics
Best Conditions: Turn of high tide during warmer months
Check designated weir safety and exclusion zones.
Burdekin Estuary Tidal Channel

Plantation Creek

Quiet mangrove creeks offering dependable mud crabbing and bread-and-butter species including Bream and Javelin Fish (Grunter). Excellent spot for small tinnie or bank work.

Top Baits/Lures: Strip mullet, peeled prawns, squid
Best Conditions: Moderate tides; incoming flow
Sandbars shift rapidly after wet season rains.
Wetland Preserve RAMSAR Listed

Bowling Green Bay National Park

Extensive saltpans and tidal creeks between Ayr and Townsville. Famed for King Threadfin Salmon, big Barramundi, and mud crabbing during the build-up months.

Top Baits/Lures: Live prawn, 20g–30g soft vibes
Best Conditions: Last 3 hours of run-out tide
Saltpan crossings bog easily when wet.
Queensland Fisheries Regulations: East Coast Barramundi closed season applies annually from midday 1 November to midday 1 February. Mud crab bag limit is 7 per person (males only, minimum carapace width 15cm).
